Who We Are

Wrapstar Leads the Way in Vehicle Protection and Style

Wrapstar Las Vegas sets the benchmark for luxury vehicle protection and customization. We were founded with a single goal, to raise the standard of paint-protection and custom automotive care in Las Vegas. Instead of relying on generic shortcuts or bulk-cut templates, we approach every project with hands-on precision and meticulous attention to detail, ensuring every panel, curve, and edge is finished to perfection.

As a proud Ceramic Pro Elite Dealer, Wrapstar provides clients with access to industry-leading protective solutions, including advanced Ceramic Pro coatings, self-healing paint-protection films (PPF), and KAVACA premium window tints. This elite partnership means every installation is performed to the highest standards and supported by Ceramic Pro’s nationwide warranty program.

Over the years, Wrapstar has earned a reputation as Las Vegas’ premier destination for high-end and exotic vehicles. Discerning clients entrust us with Bugatti, Lamborghini, Ferrari, Pagani, Koenigsegg, Rolls-Royce, Aston Martin, Bentley, Maserati, Porsche, McLaren, and other top luxury brands, because they know we provide the care, craftsmanship, and discretion their investments deserve.
